## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an AI-informed workforce intelligence framework
built using the Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025 dataset.

The objective is to help CHROs, CTOs, and enterprise talent leaders
redesign hiring, compensation, retention, and upskilling strategies in
an AI-driven ecosystem.

The analysis evaluates:

-   AI adoption intensity
-   Skill breadth across technologies
-   Compensation benchmarking
-   Workforce maturity
-   Job satisfaction & retention risk
-   AI threat perception

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🎯 Problem Statement
 
Which technical skills, tools, and development environments are shaping
the future of employability, and how should organizations prioritize
hiring and upskilling investments in an AI-enhanced ecosystem?

## 🔍 Key Insights

1.  **AI Adoption has crossed the structural threshold** -- 55.7% are AI
    power-users.
2.  **AI divide is emerging** -- 16% remain AI-agnostic.
3.  **Entry-level developers lead AI integration.**
4.  **Skill breadth (avg 7 technologies) is baseline expectation.**
5.  **82% compensation premium** exists between entry and senior
    cohorts.
6.  Skill depth predicts compensation beyond tenure alone.
7.  AI power-users show measurable productivity multiplier.
8.  Job satisfaction remains stable across AI tiers.
9.  Low AI threat perception but potential retention risk.
10. Sector concentration may create talent scarcity risk.
